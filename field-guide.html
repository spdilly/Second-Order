A SECOND ORDER FIELD GUIDE · NORTHWEST ARKANSAS

Companies reach first financing at the peer rate and reach a larger round at about two thirds of it.

A metro of 622,000 hosts Walmart, Tyson Foods, and J.B. Hunt. Companies founded there refile with the SEC within 36 months at 0.218 against a peer rate of 0.176, and they step up to a larger offering band at 0.21 against a peer band of 0.33 to 0.35. Outside firms priced every disclosed dollar raised under a single lead investor, none of the 88 enumerated Arkansas fund vehicles is mandated to lead a Series A or B, and four documented exits have produced zero validated paths from exit proceeds back into local investing.

THE FINDINGS

01

First financing reaches Northwest Arkansas companies at the peer rate.

Form D filers based in the metro refile within a fixed 36-month window at 0.218 excluding real estate, against 0.176 for peer states over the same window. On the strict treatment the two rates converge at 0.246 and 0.253. With real estate included the metro rate falls to 0.138 against 0.163, so parity depends on how real-estate filings are treated and all three treatments are published together.

02

Progression to a larger round runs at about two thirds of the peer rate under every treatment.

Step-up within 36 months reads 0.206 excluding real estate, 0.212 on the strict treatment, and 0.222 with real estate included. Peer states read 0.336, 0.348, and 0.333. A separate chain-level cohort agrees, with 2 of 46 matured Northwest Arkansas issuers stepping up against 124 of 792 for peers at 15.7 percent, a Fisher exact p of 0.020 one-sided and 0.034 two-sided, and the metro ranks below 14 of 15 peer metros on both series.

Falsification, written in advance

Five public events would move the reading, and each carries a measured baseline so that movement is legible against the instrument that set it.

  • A locally mandated Arkansas fund sole-leads a Series A or B. The baseline is zero in the 12-round sourced set, with one co-lead. Read by mid-2028.
  • A new Arkansas-based fund leads a growth round. The baseline is zero of the 88 enumerated vehicles. Read by mid-2027 on a Reg D enumeration refresh.
  • A validated edge runs from exit proceeds into local investing. The baseline is zero of six validated edges, rescanned annually.
  • A second serial founder or a documented talent spin-off appears. The baseline is one serial founder.
  • Fixed-horizon step-up converges toward the peer band. The baseline is 0.21 to 0.22 for the metro against 0.33 to 0.35 for peers, re-run annually on the same method.

Two or more locally led growth rounds arriving by mid-2028 without a new lead fund would weaken the local-capital reading. Institutional formation with no movement in step-up or reinvestment would weaken the proposed mechanism from the other side. Both directions were written down before either could occur.

Three limits the guide states about itself

The metro-level panel has no out-of-sample explanatory power for the later $10M outcome, with a state-grouped cross-validated R-squared of negative 0.05. A growth-lead mandate is therefore labeled a hypothesis and carries no demonstrated causal weight.

Under a heuristic vehicle screen the progression test weakens to 2 of 43 against 93 of 664, with a one-sided p of 0.054. The strict-screen significance claim is withdrawn at that point, and the descriptive gap of 4 to 5 percent against 14 to 16 percent stands.

The zero reinvestment count is a logged null from a four-query scan run in July 2026. Two of the four exits closed in 2024 and 2025, and the count is rescanned annually to separate recency from persistence.

Method

The guide measures five stages, running from anchor-industry operators to founders, first capital, larger rounds, and recycled capital, and gives each stage its own instrument and denominator. Access and progression run on Form D filer cohorts inside a fixed 36-month window at quarter granularity, which puts every cohort on one clock and removes right-censoring. Each rate is published under three composition treatments so that the real-estate share of Arkansas filings cannot drive the reading.

Comparison runs against peer-state filer cohorts of 21,571 filings excluding real estate, against 15 peer metros on the chain-level series, and against an independent 50-metro benchmark panel scoring seven spokes as within-panel percentiles. Robustness testing re-estimates each claim under several alternative constructions and publishes all of them, including an 87-vehicle exclusion sensitivity on the fund enumeration, leave-one-peer-out re-estimation of expected progression events, and four Form D universe definitions. Every page names its evidence class, and a sourced set of 12 rounds, a coded sample of 16 companies, and a directional peer set are never stated as market-wide rates.
