SECOND ORDER

An independent strategic finance advisory firm.

THE FIRM

Independent advice on matters of strategic and financial significance.

Second Order was founded in 2022 and operates from Little Rock, Arkansas. The firm advises privately held companies and their owners at the points where capital, ownership, or credit changes hands.

Every engagement is conducted by the firm's Principal, and the analysis a client receives is the analysis he built.
