PRINCIPAL

Sean Dillard

Principal, Second Order

Sean Dillard founded Second Order in 2022 and conducts the firm's engagements across capital formation, mergers and acquisitions, credit facilities, and private fund economics. He is also a Managing Director, Capital Advisory at Nassau Street Partners, where he advises owners on the sale of their companies.

He began his career at Boston Consulting Group, where he owned the indirect procurement workstream of a Fortune 500 cost transformation. He then joined Red Ventures, where he launched a digital health product inside the Healthgrades physician marketplace and grew it to approximately $15 million in annual revenue, running the pharma-partnered marketing programs and the physician-targeting algorithm behind it.

His work at Second Order covers SBA and commercial lender packages, growth-equity operating models, distribution waterfalls for private funds, acquisition underwriting, and pricing and revenue management. He chaired the Urban Development vertical of the Stanford GSB Impact Fund.

He holds an MBA from the Stanford Graduate School of Business and a Bachelor of Science in mathematics from Duke University. He lives in Little Rock, Arkansas.
