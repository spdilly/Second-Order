ABOUT

An independent strategic finance advisory firm.

Second Order advises founders, owners, and emerging fund managers on capital formation, mergers and acquisitions, credit facilities, and private fund economics.

The firm

Second Order provides independent advice on matters of strategic and financial significance to founders, chief executives, chief financial officers, and institutional investors. The work covers capital formation, mergers and acquisitions, credit facilities, private fund economics, and the operating analysis that supports a transaction.

The firm holds no capital, sells no product, and takes no position in the outcome it advises on. Its independence is the reason a client can act on the answer.

The method

Every model is verified by code-level gates before a client sees it. Balances tie, bridges close, and a figure that cannot be traced to an input fails the build. What a gate cannot decide is which case becomes the base case, and that judgment is what the firm is retained for.
